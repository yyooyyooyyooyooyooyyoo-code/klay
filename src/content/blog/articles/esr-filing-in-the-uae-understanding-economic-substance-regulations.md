# ESR Filing in the UAE – Understanding Economic Substance Regulations

ESR filing in the UAE has been an important part of the country's regulatory framework for businesses operating in specific sectors. The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) were introduced to ensure that companies carrying out certain activities maintained an appropriate level of economic presence in the UAE.

However, the regulatory position has changed. Following Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024, the UAE cancelled the requirement to submit Economic Substance Notifications and Economic Substance Reports for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.

This means that businesses should no longer treat ESR notification and reporting as a routine annual filing for current financial years. Nevertheless, companies may still have responsibilities relating to earlier financial periods, including outstanding submissions, compliance records, information requests and penalties.

Understanding this distinction is important for Mainland and Free Zone businesses, particularly those that operated during the original ESR reporting period.

This guide explains the purpose of Economic Substance Regulations, the activities covered by the original framework, the 2024 amendment, historical compliance responsibilities and what UAE businesses should consider today.

## What Are Economic Substance Regulations in the UAE?

The Economic Substance Regulations were introduced to align the UAE with international standards on tax transparency and to address concerns about businesses being established in jurisdictions without genuine economic activity.

The original framework required certain UAE companies and other business entities conducting defined Relevant Activities to demonstrate adequate economic substance in the country.

The regulations were introduced through Cabinet Resolution No. 31 of 2019 and subsequently updated through Cabinet Resolution No. 57 of 2020.

Under the original framework, businesses conducting Relevant Activities were required to assess their reporting obligations and, where applicable, demonstrate that their operations had sufficient substance in the UAE.

The framework covered businesses operating in Mainland and Free Zone jurisdictions, subject to the applicable definitions, conditions and exemptions.

## What Changed in UAE ESR Filing Requirements in 2024?

One of the most important developments for UAE businesses was Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024.

The UAE Ministry of Finance announced on 14 October 2024 that economic substance reporting requirements had been cancelled for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.

As a result:

- ESR Notifications are no longer required for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.
- Economic Substance Reports are no longer required for those financial years.
- Applicable obligations for earlier financial years remain relevant.
- Businesses must continue responding to information or amendment requests from the relevant authorities concerning earlier periods.
- Penalties imposed for earlier financial years remain subject to the applicable rules.

The amendment also addressed penalties relating to periods after the reporting cut-off.

Businesses should therefore distinguish between historical ESR compliance and current corporate regulatory obligations.

The cancellation of ESR reporting does not mean that outstanding responsibilities for earlier periods have automatically disappeared.

## Which Financial Years Are Still Relevant for ESR Compliance?

The original ESR framework applied to financial years starting on or after 1 January 2019.

Following the 2024 amendment, the relevant reporting period is generally limited to financial years ending on or before 31 December 2022.

For example:

- A company with a financial year ending on 31 December 2021 may have historical ESR obligations.
- A company with a financial year ending on 31 December 2022 may have historical ESR obligations.
- A company with a financial year ending on 31 December 2023 is not required to submit an ESR Notification or Economic Substance Report for that period.
- A company with a financial year ending on 30 June 2023 is also outside the reporting requirement under the amended cut-off.

The financial year-end is important when determining whether a reporting period falls within the historical ESR framework.

Businesses should review their actual accounting periods rather than relying only on the calendar year in which the company was incorporated.

## Which Activities Were Covered Under the Original ESR Framework?

The original Economic Substance Regulations identified nine Relevant Activities.

These activities were subject to specific definitions and conditions. A company's trade licence description alone was not necessarily sufficient to determine whether it fell within the ESR framework.

The nine Relevant Activities were:

### 1. Banking Business

Banking activities were included in the original ESR framework. Relevant businesses needed to assess their activities and applicable economic substance requirements.

### 2. Insurance Business

Insurance businesses conducting activities within the regulatory definition were covered by the original framework.

### 3. Investment Fund Management Business

Businesses managing investment funds were included, subject to the applicable definitions and conditions.

### 4. Lease-Finance Business

This activity covered certain leasing and financing arrangements falling within the ESR definition.

### 5. Headquarters Business

A company providing headquarters services to related entities could fall within the original Relevant Activity definition.

### 6. Shipping Business

Certain shipping activities were included in the original framework, depending on the nature of the business operations.

### 7. Holding Company Business

Holding companies carrying out the activities defined under ESR were included in the original Relevant Activity framework.

### 8. Intellectual Property Business

Certain businesses generating income from intellectual property assets were covered by specific ESR provisions.

### 9. Distribution and Service Centre Business

Businesses conducting qualifying distribution and service centre activities for related entities were also included.

Whether a business actually conducted a Relevant Activity depended on the detailed regulatory definitions and its real operations.

These activity categories remain useful when reviewing historical ESR exposure for financial years within the applicable reporting period.

## What Was the Difference Between ESR Notification and ESR Report?

Under the original ESR framework, the notification and report served different purposes.

### ESR Notification

The ESR Notification was an initial submission used to provide information about the entity and whether it conducted one or more Relevant Activities during the relevant financial period.

It helped the regulatory authority identify businesses potentially falling within the ESR framework.

### Economic Substance Report

The Economic Substance Report provided more detailed information about the company's activities and economic substance.

Where applicable, the report included information relating to:

- Relevant Activities.
- Relevant income.
- Management and direction in the UAE.
- Core Income-Generating Activities.
- Employees and operating expenditure.
- Physical assets and premises.
- Other information required under the original framework.

The notification and report were separate reporting requirements under the previous regime.

**Important:** These descriptions explain the historical filing framework. They should not be interpreted as a requirement to submit new ESR Notifications or Reports for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.

## What Were the Economic Substance Requirements?

Under the original regulations, businesses conducting Relevant Activities and earning Relevant Income generally needed to assess whether they met the Economic Substance Test, unless an applicable exemption or special treatment applied.

The test focused on whether the company demonstrated an appropriate level of economic presence in the UAE.

The assessment considered several areas.

### 1. Management and Direction in the UAE

The company was expected to demonstrate that relevant management and strategic decisions were directed and managed appropriately in the UAE.

This could involve reviewing board meetings, decision-making processes and supporting records.

### 2. Core Income-Generating Activities

Businesses were expected to conduct the activities that generated their relevant income in the UAE, in accordance with the requirements for their particular Relevant Activity.

### 3. Adequate Employees

The original framework considered whether the business had an adequate number of appropriately qualified employees to conduct its relevant activities.

### 4. Physical Assets and Premises

Companies were expected to maintain appropriate physical assets and premises in the UAE, depending on the nature and scale of their operations.

### 5. Operating Expenditure

The original Economic Substance Test also considered whether operating expenditure in the UAE was adequate and appropriate for the relevant business activity.

The requirements were assessed according to the nature and scale of the business. They were not identical for every company.

These historical requirements may still be relevant when reviewing compliance for financial years within the original ESR period.

## Did ESR Apply to Mainland and Free Zone Companies?

Under the original framework, ESR was not limited to Mainland companies.

Businesses in Mainland and Free Zone jurisdictions could fall within its scope if they conducted a Relevant Activity and met the applicable regulatory conditions.

### Mainland Companies

Mainland companies conducting Relevant Activities were required to assess their ESR position during the original reporting period.

Their obligations depended on their actual operations, income and any applicable exemptions.

### Free Zone Companies

Free Zone companies were also included in the original framework.

A Free Zone licence did not automatically exempt a business from ESR. The company needed to review its activities and the applicable regulatory conditions.

### Offshore and Other Business Structures

The original regulations also contained definitions and provisions relevant to different types of UAE business entities.

The applicable treatment depended on the legal form, activity and regulatory circumstances.

The 2024 amendment changed the reporting position for financial years ending after 31 December 2022, regardless of whether a company operated in Mainland or Free Zone jurisdictions.

## What Happens If a Company Missed Historical ESR Filing?

Although new ESR reporting is no longer required for financial years ending after 31 December 2022, businesses should not ignore historical compliance responsibilities.

A company that was within the scope of the original framework may need to review whether it completed the applicable submissions for earlier financial years.

Historical issues may include:

- Missing ESR Notifications.
- Missing Economic Substance Reports.
- Incorrect information in previous submissions.
- Incomplete records supporting the Economic Substance Test.
- Outstanding authority requests.
- Penalties imposed for historical reporting periods.

The Federal Tax Authority may still deal with applicable historical compliance matters.

Businesses should review their records and obtain professional advice where historical submissions or penalties remain unresolved.

The exact records required depend on the company's historical activities and circumstances.

Businesses should also preserve relevant documentation where an authority has requested information or where an outstanding compliance matter remains unresolved.

## Does ESR Cancellation Mean UAE Businesses Have No Compliance Responsibilities?

No. The cancellation of ESR reporting for financial years ending after 31 December 2022 does not remove other UAE business compliance obligations.

Companies may still need to comply with requirements relating to:

- UAE Corporate Tax.
- VAT, where applicable.
- Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing requirements.
- Ultimate Beneficial Ownership information.
- Trade licence renewals.
- Accounting and record keeping.
- Applicable sector-specific regulations.
- Regulatory approvals and reporting.

These are separate regulatory frameworks and should not be confused with ESR filing.

For example, a company may no longer need to submit an ESR Notification for a current financial year but may still have Corporate Tax registration, filing and payment responsibilities depending on its circumstances.

## Common ESR Compliance Mistakes Businesses Should Avoid

### 1. Assuming ESR Was Completely Erased

The 2024 amendment removed reporting requirements for financial years ending after 31 December 2022. It did not automatically remove all historical responsibilities.

### 2. Ignoring Earlier Financial Years

Companies should review whether they had Relevant Activities during the original reporting period and whether applicable submissions were completed.

### 3. Confusing ESR With Corporate Tax

Economic Substance Regulations and UAE Corporate Tax are separate regulatory frameworks. Compliance with one does not automatically satisfy the requirements of the other.

### 4. Assuming Free Zone Companies Were Automatically Exempt

Free Zone entities were included in the original ESR framework where the relevant conditions were met.

### 5. Failing to Maintain Historical Records

Companies should retain appropriate supporting documentation for earlier periods, particularly where reporting or authority requests remain outstanding.

### 6. Relying on Outdated Filing Information

Some online articles and regulatory summaries still describe ESR Notifications and Reports as annual obligations without explaining the 2024 amendment.

Businesses should confirm the current position using the latest official information rather than relying on outdated filing instructions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Who needed to submit ESR filing in the UAE?

Under the original framework, UAE businesses conducting one or more Relevant Activities were required to assess their ESR obligations. Depending on their circumstances, they could be required to submit an ESR Notification and an Economic Substance Report.

The framework applied to qualifying Mainland and Free Zone entities.

### 2. Is ESR filing still required in the UAE?

ESR Notifications and Economic Substance Reports are no longer required for financial years ending after 31 December 2022, following Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024.

Historical obligations for earlier financial years remain relevant.

### 3. What is the difference between ESR Notification and ESR Report?

An ESR Notification provided initial information about the entity and its Relevant Activities. An Economic Substance Report contained more detailed information about the company's activities, relevant income and economic substance.

These were separate requirements under the original framework.

### 4. Are Free Zone companies subject to ESR requirements?

Free Zone companies were included in the original ESR framework if they conducted Relevant Activities and met the applicable conditions.

The reporting requirement has since been removed for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.

### 5. Does ESR apply to Mainland companies?

Mainland companies were also covered by the original ESR framework when they conducted Relevant Activities and met the applicable conditions.

Historical reporting obligations should be reviewed according to the company's financial year-end.

### 6. What are the nine Relevant Activities under ESR?

The original framework covered banking, insurance, investment fund management, lease-finance, headquarters, shipping, holding company, intellectual property, and distribution and service centre businesses.

### 7. What happens if a company did not submit an ESR Report for an earlier financial year?

If a company was required to submit a report for a historical period ending on or before 31 December 2022 and did not do so, it should review the outstanding matter and seek appropriate professional guidance.

The 2024 amendment did not automatically remove historical filing responsibilities.

### 8. Can the UAE authorities still request historical ESR information?

Yes. The Ministry of Finance confirmed that companies remain responsible for responding to information or amendment requests from the relevant regulatory authorities or the Federal Tax Authority relating to earlier periods.

### 9. Does the cancellation of ESR reporting mean companies no longer need to maintain economic substance?

The cancellation concerns the ESR notification and reporting requirements for financial years ending after 31 December 2022. Businesses must still comply with other applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

The term economic substance may also be relevant in other tax and regulatory contexts, but those requirements should be assessed separately.

### 10. Which decision changed the UAE ESR reporting requirements?

Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024 amended certain provisions of Cabinet Decision No. 57 of 2020 concerning Economic Substance Requirements. The Ministry of Finance announced the cancellation of reporting requirements for financial years ending after 31 December 2022 on 14 October 2024.

## Final Thoughts: Understanding ESR Filing and UAE Compliance Requirements

ESR filing was an important part of the UAE's regulatory framework for businesses conducting specific Relevant Activities.

However, the 2024 amendment changed the reporting position significantly. Businesses are no longer required to submit ESR Notifications or Economic Substance Reports for financial years ending after 31 December 2022.

The focus for companies today should be on understanding whether they have any outstanding responsibilities for earlier financial years, maintaining relevant historical records and meeting their current regulatory obligations.

For Mainland and Free Zone businesses, understanding the difference between historical ESR requirements and current UAE compliance frameworks is essential.
